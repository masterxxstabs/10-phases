# Changelog

Each version's notes become its GitHub Release text, and the first few lines
show on the in-game update card. Add a section here when you bump the version
in `ten_phases/__init__.py`.

## 1.11.0

- New game: Monopoly! Arrow over to it on the main menu, pick your token and how many rounds to play
- Everything is animated: dice tumble, tokens hop square by square (or fly to jail), cards flip, coins fly and houses pop up. Hold Space to speed it up
- Buy, auction, build houses and hotels, mortgage, and trade with anyone. Computers from Easy to Expert bargain back
- Works in multiplayer for up to 6 players: pick MONOPOLY in the lobby (everyone needs this version to play together)

## 1.10.0

- New game: Cards Against Humanity! Arrow over to it on the main menu (it's for adults)
- Pick exactly which of the 71 packs go in the deck, how many blank cards are shuffled in, and the points to win
- Blank cards let you write your own answer, and you can put a picture on them too (or turn pictures off)
- Works in multiplayer: pick CAH in the lobby. Cards download once from the REST Against Humanity API, then work offline

## 1.9.0

- New password-protected admin panel for the game's owner, opened from any card table
- Admin tools: see every player's cards, give any player a card, and kick guests from games you host
- Small fixes to the test setup

## 1.8.0

- New game: Texas Hold'em Poker! Arrow over to POKER on the main menu
- Choose exactly how many chips to bet every round: drag the slider, tap - and +, or use MIN, 1/2 POT, POT and ALL IN
- Computer players from Easy (calls everything) to Expert (bluffs and steals the blinds), plus a Hint that tells you your chance to win
- Poker works in multiplayer too: pick POKER in the lobby (everyone needs this version to play together)

## 1.7.0

- Card packs! Skins now come from Basic, Premium and Legendary packs that you rip open with a swipe
- Flip your cards one by one: rare cards glow before you flip them, and legendaries get the full show
- Almost 300 skins: new hand-made ones (including the Koi Pond set), plus a huge generated collection with lots of foil finishes
- Duplicates turn back into chips, and Classic, Bubble, Minimal, Checker and Racing Stripes are free for everyone
- Your skins show up for everyone in multiplayer (everyone needs this version to play together)
- Bug fixes: the host can't be crashed by bad network messages, damaged settings files no longer stop the game, slot wins are kept if you close mid-spin, and more

## 1.6.0

- New Card Shop: spend your Chip Bank on card skins (6 card fronts, 7 card backs) from the SHOP button
- New Lucky 10 Slots: a single-player slot machine that bets real Chip Bank chips (jackpot pays 300x!)
- Skins show up in every game, and even on the slot machine's jackpot symbol

## 1.5.0

- New game: Blackjack! Arrow over to it on the main menu
- Beat the dealer over 10 hands: hit, stand, double down and split, with blackjack paying 3:2
- Computer players from Easy to Expert (Expert counts cards!) and a Hint that explains the perfect move
- Blackjack works in multiplayer too: pick it in the lobby

## 1.4.0

- Go Fish multiplayer! Pick 10 PHASES or GO FISH in the lobby, then invite friends the same way
- Nearby games and the lobby show which game you're joining
- Everyone needs this version to play together online

## 1.3.0

- New game: Go Fish! Use the arrows on the main menu to switch between 10 Phases and Go Fish
- Go Fish has the same look, computer players from Easy to Expert, hints, and speech bubbles
- Every Go Fish book you make adds 5 chips to your Chip Bank

## 1.2.2

- Hint fixes: it now spots phases where two sets share a number (like 4444+W and 4+W)
- The Hint never tells you to discard a card you need for your phase
- "Take the discard" is only suggested when that card actually gets you closer

## 1.2.1

- Computer players now get random names from a pool of 70, so every game has a different cast

## 1.2.0

- Hover over any player to see their phase, cards on the table, chips and recent moves
- Easier-to-read pixel text with a new bundled pixel font
- Chips you finish a game with are saved to your Chip Bank on the main menu

## 1.1.0

- The game now updates itself: new versions download in the background and install with one click
- Your version number shows on the main menu and in Settings
- Multiplayer tells you when you and the host are on different versions
- New Settings option to turn automatic updates off

## 1.0.0

- First release: Phase 10-style card game with Easy to Expert computer players
- Handheld-style table, sound effects and a Customize screen
- Multiplayer with invite codes and nearby games
